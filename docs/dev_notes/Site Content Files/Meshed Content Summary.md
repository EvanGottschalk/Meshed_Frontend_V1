Meshed Overview

Meshed is a network intelligence layer for investment communities. It uses AI and data science to help VCs, family offices, private equity firms, and curated ecosystems discover high-value connections across their portfolio or member base.

Meshed is a human-verified coordination and value distribution layer for founders, employees, and agents.

Instead of proving that a data event was attested externally, Meshed now proves that important interactions were initiated, accepted, or completed by verified humans.

Feature: Informed Agents Overview

Meshed agents make highly informed, optimally customized decisions automatically by learning context from connected accounts.

Users connect their social media accounts, ChatGPT or other LLM accounts, Slack accounts, work email accounts, work documentation, Github pushes, etc., and Meshed agents read them to understand context. Meshed agents then use this context to make matches between companies, investors, and employees. Meshed agents automatically keep up with development. Meshed agents can create business plans, find valuable partnerships, and send correspondence automatically, vastly reducing the extra work investors, employers, and employees need to do.

Efficient Context Questions

Meshed AI agents automatically generate high-value, non-repetitive questions to ask users. Answering these questions keeps the agents up-to-date on context, and allow them to use automation to complete more tasks.

Since agents can see social posts, ChatGPT conversations, Slack messages, emails, docs, Github pushes, etc., they generate questions for users to fill in context, and never need to be informed about “the obvious.”

“Repeating yourself” is completely eliminated - users never need to re-communicate information that they have already written or read elsewhere.

Agents will always avoid redundancy - users won't need to repeat information, just provide new information.

Example agent questions:

* "I read on Slack that you did XYZ. Did you hit any obstacles?"
  * It only asks that if there was no mention of obstacles on connected platforms
* "I saw you and [colleague] did XYZ. What were their contributions?"
  * It only asks that if who did what wasn't made clear on connected platforms

Privacy & Data Security

* Locally Store Sensitive Data - users do not share their sensitive data with Meshed servers or databases. Information read and created by Meshed agents is saved locally within an individual’s or organization’s hardware.
* Never Store Personal Private Data - users do not share their emails, messages, or other private information with Meshed servers or databases. Meshed agents create instructions from private information, such as emails, by reading them in encrypted temporary local environments. None of the information is saved anywhere.

Features by User Story

1. VC & LP

Portfolio Optimization - Meshed makes it easy for investors to optimize their investments using AI.

* In any given portfolio of companies, some companies are facing challenges, whereas other companies have faced and overcome those challenges. Meshed agents study and organize data about companies in one’s portfolio, and matches those in need with those who have the solutions.

Contact Automation - Meshed automatically connects investors with key individuals and partners.

* Investors are constantly working to maintain contact with all of the valuable people in their network, and to find new high-value contacts. Meshed agents study and organize data about individuals, investors, and companies to find optimal matches with investors. Meshed agents automatically create succinct summaries about contacts, and can automatically send messages and calendar invites on a user’s behalf.

3. Individual

“ChatGPT for Finding Work” - Meshed makes it easy for people to find jobs.

* Since Meshed agents have deep contextual understanding of many different businesses and investors, they can identify ideal employers for those seeking work, where the fit is ideal for both the employer and employee.
* By connecting their accounts, Meshed agents can automatically create great resumes and cover letters for individuals, and send them out on the individual’s behalf.

Income Optimization - Meshed makes it easy for people to land the best-paying jobs possible.

* Since Meshed agents have deep contextual understanding of many different businesses and investors, they can identify ideal target employers for those seeking work, and give guidance on how to achieve those higher paying jobs. This may involve recommendations of courses, guidance on how to reach out to target employers, and modifications to an individual’s resume.

Progressive Feedback - Meshed gives feedback on your job search.

* As individuals apply to jobs, receive responses, and interview, Meshed agents track their progress. Meshed agents then give informed feedback to users on how to have more success.
* Other job search platforms leave it all up to the user, offering only the most basic tools for searching and applying. Others offer no guidance or feedback as the user continues to apply and improve.
